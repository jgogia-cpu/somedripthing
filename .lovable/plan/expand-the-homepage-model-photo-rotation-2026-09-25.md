# Expand the homepage model-photo rotation

## Goal
Use every existing campaign/lookbook image that visibly includes a person or model in the homepage cover rotation, rather than limiting it to eight selected photos.

## Changes
- Expand the curated model-photo list across all eligible brand lookbooks.
- Keep logos, isolated products, flat lays, and product-only photos out of the rotation.
- Remove the eight-slide cap so the homepage cycles through the full curated set in a shuffled order.
- Keep forbidden brands excluded and preserve the current automatic rotation, arrows, labels, and links.

## Verification
- Confirm the slide count reflects the expanded set.
- Cycle through the full rotation and check every image loads and belongs to the displayed brand.
- Check the homepage at mobile and desktop sizes and confirm the project remains error-free.
