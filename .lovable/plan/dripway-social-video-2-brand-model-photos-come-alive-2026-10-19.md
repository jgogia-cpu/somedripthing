# DRIPWAY social video #2 — brand model photos come alive

A second vertical (9:16) social video, made in a different style from the first one. Instead of a phone with scrolling screens and animated text, this one uses AI-generated motion: real lookbook photos of models from the featured brands are brought to life as short cinematic clips, then cut together into one fast, moody edit.

## What it looks like
- About 20–25 seconds, tall format for Reels / TikTok / Shorts.
- 4–5 short clips, each starting from a real model photo from a different brand's lookbook (for example Preview Worldwide, Harvx, Mortenoir, Maker, Redeem or Die, City of Saints). Each clip adds subtle motion only — a slow camera push-in, a head turn, fabric moving, light shifting — while keeping the outfit, face and clothing unchanged.
- Fast cuts between brands with a dark, heavy beat, matching the black-and-bone look of the site.
- Short bold captions in Anton uppercase between shots (for example "THE BRANDS YOU HAVEN'T FOUND YET"), each brand's name shown on its clip, and an end card with the DRIPWAY logo and thedripway.com.
- Apollo Originals, Christopher Noir and VeroBottega are never used.

## Steps
1. Choose 4–5 of the strongest model photos (sharp, full outfit, person clearly visible) from the brand lookbooks.
2. Generate one short motion clip per photo, each with one camera move and a "keep the person and clothing unchanged" instruction.
3. Join the clips with fast cuts, add the captions, brand names, music and the DRIPWAY end card, then check frames throughout before sharing.
4. Save the result to Files as `videos/dripway-brand-models-9x16.mp4`, without touching the website.

## Note
Each generated clip uses AI video credits (one per brand shot, about 4–5 in total).
